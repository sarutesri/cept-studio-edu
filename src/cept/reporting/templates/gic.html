1. Method

Geomagnetically induced current (GIC) is a quasi-DC phenomenon. OpenDSS models the geo-electric field as voltage sources in the transmission lines (GICLine) and solves the resulting DC network at a low frequency ({{ frequency }} Hz). Transformer neutral-to-ground paths (GICTransformer + grounding reactors) carry the GIC.
